Therefore, the Home Front Command approved the cinematic event to take place, under the proper safety guidelines, of course.\nResidents evacuated from their homes in the South and the North are eligible for free admission tickets to the screenings. – for additional information\nThe event administrative staff said: “During these difficult and complex times, the goal is to create a meetup place of unity, refuge and hope, to give the audience a chance to disconnect for a few hours from everything that is happening around them and enable the art of cinema to speak. We cannot offer adequate consolation for those who were hurt or uprooted from their homes, but we can offer a home that is not just a roof over their heads, but a community life that among other things, encourages artistic creation and inspiration. During the cinematic event, as is during its preparation and every single day since the war began, our hearts are with the bereaved families and the families whose loved ones were kidnapped. We will not forget or stop thinking about our children, brothers and sisters held hostage, our hearts are with them, and we join in the urgent call to take any action necessary for their safe return home.”\nZuqim Arava\n## Arriving to the Festival\n  * For those arriving from the North | South \n  * Parking and Access to the Screening Complex\n  * Safety Guidelines\n\n\n## Tickets\n### Purchase
