The plot focuses on three characters: A young single father striving to find the strength to love; a feminist librarian, single by choice, who has decided not to become a mother; and a six-year-old child seeking a place in a new family structure. Through their aspirations, fears, and choices, the director portrays the diverse ways in which people create and define a family.
