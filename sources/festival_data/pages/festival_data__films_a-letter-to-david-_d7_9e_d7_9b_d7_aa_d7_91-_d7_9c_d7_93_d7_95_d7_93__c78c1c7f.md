Filmmaker Tom Shoval crafts a personal cinematic letter to David Cunio, who was abducted from Nir Oz to Gaza on October 7th and was released after 738 days in captivity. Ten years ago, David and his twin brother Eitan starred in Shoval’s award-winning debut feature film  _Youth_(Berlinale 2013), which focused on the powerful bond between brothers and, in a tragic and unimaginable twist of fate, revolved around a kidnapping. Through unedited behind-the-scenes footage and audition tapes from Youth, Shoval builds a multi-layered documentary that explores the inexplicable connections between life and cinema, memory and reality, and the catastrophic consequences of war.

### Producer
**Gal Greenspan**
Gal Greenspan is an award-winning film and television producer, originally from Tel Aviv and now based in Melbourne. A graduate of the Sam Spiegel Film & Television School in Jerusalem, he founded Green Productions in 2009 and has produced internationally recognised films and series premiering at Cannes, Sundance, TIFF, Berlinale and other major festivals. His work spans more than 30 films, series and documentaries, developed and produced across Europe, North America and Australasia, with a strong focus on ambitious, globally oriented storytelling. Greenspan is Co-Managing Director of MOFA and leads MOFA Stories, the company’s long-form film and television slate. Selected credits include Scaffolding (Cannes), Menashe (Sundance), Second To None (Max), Marama (TIFF), Red Alert (Paramount+), and Tell Me Everything (Sundance).
