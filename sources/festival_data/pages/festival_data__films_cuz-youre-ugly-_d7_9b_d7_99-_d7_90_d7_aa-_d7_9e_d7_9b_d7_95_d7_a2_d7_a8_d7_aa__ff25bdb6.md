#####  Avigail, a wild and insecure midshipwoman, returns home to the stuffyJerusalem apartment she shares with her dysfunctional mother and her 15-year-old, naïve-looking sister. Avigail hopes to untie the chains of childhood and lose her virginity over the weekend. But her return takes a sharp turn: her young sister is pregnant. Now, instead of getting rid of her virginity, she must get rid of a baby.

### Director
**Sharon Angelhart**
Sharon Angelhart is a scriptwriter and director. After developing the script for her debut feature Cuz You’re Ugly at the Cannes Cinéfondation Residence, the film premiered at the Jerusalem Film Festival and was nominated for seven Israeli Academy Awards, winning two. Her short films have screened at numerous international festivals, including Cannes, Jerusalem, and Sarajevo. She is currently developing her second feature, I Hate You from the Bottom of My Heart, which was developed at Berlin AiR, supported by Medienboard Berlin-Brandenburg
