#### Eid, a young man from Rahat harboring a childhood trauma, escapes into the world of playwriting while secretly yearning for a married actress in Paris. When his family forces him into a marriage, he negotiates an unconventional deal with his wife – a child in exchange for freedom. But a disheartening trip to Paris forces him to confront his past and fight for his true desires within the confines of his traditional Bedouin society.
#### Followed by Q&A with Director Yosef Abu Mdegem

### Director
**Yosef Abu Mdegem**
Yosef Abu Mdegem is a director, social activist, and a teacher. He holds a bachelor's degree and a master's degree from the School of Arts at Sapir College. Graduate of the Mandel Center for Young Leadership in Rahat. A film and media teacher, entrepreneur, and social activist: "Creators from Rahat and Cinema Breaks Boundaries", a film and television project in Rahat, with the support of the Sapir Film Department. Directed numerous short documentaries that screened in Israel and abroad. Winner of the Best Director award in the Arab sector - Keshet broadcasting.
