Spanning from the Yom Kippur War to the horrors of October 7th, Major General Doron Almog’s life is a timeline of Israeli tragedy. Yet, surrounded by five decades of bereavement, this Israel Prize laureate refuses to despair. A profound portrait of a leader transforming relentless personal grief into a legacy of national hope.
Followed by Q&A with The Jewish Agency for Israel CEO and Former Major General **Doron Almog**
