##### Spending 15 years apart from her family in order to build their future, Mila has become comfortable in the wealthy landscape of her employer. When an accident sends her unexpectedly back home to rural Poland, Mila has to face a new truth: her husband and daughter’s lives have been moving on without her.

### Director
**Or Sinai**
Or Sinai is a Sam Spiegel Film School graduate and Berlinale Talents alumna. Her debut feature Mama received international funding and awards, including Torino Script Lab and CNC prizes. Her short film Anna won the Cannes Cinéfondation Award and the Israeli Film Academy Award, among others.
