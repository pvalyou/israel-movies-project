##### Weeks after giving birth to her first child, a young religious woman arrives at her Rabbi’s wife’s home, claiming her husband has been replaced. He looks identical, but she insists he’s a double, yet fears institutionalization if she seeks police help. The Rabbi’s wife believes she needs psychiatric care, but the Rabbi interprets it as taking a Midrash too literally, expecting it to resolve itself. From there, everything spirals. This multi-character mystery drama explores how people change in relationships, the difference between falling in love and staying in love, and what must be endured between them.
##### Followed by a Q&A with Director David Tauber and Producer Eliya Tauber

### Director
**David Tauber**
David Tauber is a director, screenwriter, and producer with a strong passion for cinema and a proven track record of creating award-winning films. He graduated with honors from the Ma'aleh School of Film in 2012 and is the founder of Colorados Productions, specializing in high-quality, low-budget film productions. 
  * ![](https://israelfilmfestival.com/wp-content/themes/Tyco/assets/images/placeholder.png)
### Producer
**Eliya Tauber**
Eliya Tauber. Film producer and the founder of Colorados Productions. I produce short films and documentary films supported by Israeli film funds, including the independent feature film "My One and Only." I am also active in film education, studied professional pastry, and teach pastry at an institution for girls at risk. I live in Jerusalem with my husband and four sons.
