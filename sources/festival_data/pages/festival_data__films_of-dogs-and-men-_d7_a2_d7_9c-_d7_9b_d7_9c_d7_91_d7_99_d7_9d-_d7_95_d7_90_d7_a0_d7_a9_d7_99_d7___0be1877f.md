##### As dawn breaks, 16-year-old Dar (played by actress Ori Avinoam) returns to her kibbutz in search of her missing dog, lost in the attacks she survived just days before. She confronts the aftermath of the massacre in her community, and the horrors etched in the faces of the people around her, while witnessing the stark reality of the tragedy unfolding across the fence in Gaza. Between those who seek revenge and those whose faith in mankind remains unwavering, Dar tries to find her own voice.

### Director
**Dani Rosenberg**
Dani Rosenberg is a graduate of the Sam Spiegel Film School in Jerusalem. His debut feature, The Death of Cinema and My Father’s Too, premiered in the Official Selection at Cannes 2020 and won the Jerusalem Film Festival. His subsequent work has screened at major international festivals, including Locarno, Berlinale, IDFA, and Hot Docs. "Of Dogs and Men" is his third feature film.
