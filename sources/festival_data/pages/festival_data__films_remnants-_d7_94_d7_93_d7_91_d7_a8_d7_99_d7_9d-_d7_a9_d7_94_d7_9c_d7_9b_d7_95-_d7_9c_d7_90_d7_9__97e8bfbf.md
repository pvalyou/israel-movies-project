Dir: Roman Shumunov 
After losing her sister on the October 7th terror attack, Rona withdraws into grief and anger. When her school rejects her way of commemorating her sister at the Memorial Day ceremony, she teams up with classmate Oren to ensure her sister is remembered the way she wanted – with her music.
Followed by Q&A with Creator Eden Gurion

### Creator
**Eden Gurion**
Eden Gurion is an award-winning Israeli writer and creator of films and youth series, including Remnants, which explores the aftermath of the October 7 events, and Palmach Rising. His work has received BANFF Rockie Awards and Israeli Television Academy honors.
