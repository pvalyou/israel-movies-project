Dir: Eli Cohen

1986 
In loving memory of Alon Aboutboul, this special screening is a tribute to an extraordinary artist whose presence shaped generations of Israeli cinema. Marking 40 years since the film’s release, _Ricochets_ stands as a powerful testament to Alon’s depth, sensitivity, and enduring legacy. Set in Lebanon in 1986, the film follows a platoon of Israeli soldiers on the eve of Israel’s withdrawal, capturing the fear, doubt, and moral weight of fighting a guerrilla war amid civilian life. Through Alon Abutbul’s unforgettable performance, _Ricochets_ remains a deeply human portrait of resilience and of an artist whose voice continues to resonate.
