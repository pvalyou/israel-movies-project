2025 Ophir Awards\n**![](https://www.filmfund.org.il/Upload/Media/Tinymce/Pictures/18_10_08_54.jpg)**(Naama Greenbaum)\n**[The Sea](https://intl.filmfund.org.il/Movie?movieId=20)** , directed by **Shai Carmeli-Pollak** , was awarded the Ophir Award for Best Feature Film of 2025.\nProduced by **Baher Agbariya** - Majdal Productions, **The Sea** claimed four additional Ophir Awards — Best Screenplay, Best Actor (Muhammad Gazawi), Best Supporting Actor (Khalifa Natour), and Best Original Score (Avi Belleli) — bringing its tally to five. The film has also been selected as Israel’s official submission for the Best International Feature category at the Academy Awards.\n![](https://www.filmfund.org.il/Upload/Media/Tinymce/Pictures/18_10_10_25.jpg)(Naama Greenbaum)\n**Eti Tsiko** 's **[Nandaori](https://intl.filmfund.org.il/Movie?movieId=30)** film won the Ophir Award for Best Director, as well as Best Actress (Neta Riskin) Best Cinematography (Shai Goldman), and the awards for Best Costume Design and Best Makeup.\n![](https://www.filmfund.org.il/Upload/Media/Tinymce/Pictures/18_10_11_55.jpg)(Naama Greenbaum**)**\nIn addition, [**Cuz You’re Ugly**](https://intl.filmfund.org.il/Movie?movieId=29), directed by **Sharon Angelhart** , won Best Supporting Actress (Uri Avinoam) and Best Casting. [**Yes!**](https://intl.filmfund.org.il/Movie?movieId=2), directed by **Nadav Lapid** , received the awards for Best Editing (Nili Feller) and Best Soundtrack.\nOverall, films supported by the Israel Film Fund won**14 Ophir Awards** , including Best Feature Film.\nIsrael Film Fund\n(RA)\n12 Yehudit Blvd. Tel Aviv - Yafo
