OLIVE Awards | פרסי הזית | جوائز الزيتون\n**OLIVE Awards | פרסי הזית | جوائز الزيتون**\n![](https://www.filmfund.org.il/Upload/Media/Tinymce/Pictures/25_10_48_27.jpg)\n* * *\nThe**Olive Awards** , supported by**Great Hill** , are grounded in a belief in cinema as a space of artistic freedom, courage, and excellence. The awards support outstanding films shaped by a wide range of voices and cinematic approaches, guided by a clear artistic vision.\nCommitted to freedom of creation, the Olive Awards reject censorship in all its forms and welcome films that engage with complexity, tension, and challenging ideas. Films are selected on the basis of artistic merit, free from political influence or external pressure. In this spirit, Great Hill supports outstanding films, honoring the power of cinema to question, to challenge, and to illuminate.\nThe Olive Awards will distribute a total of **$250,000** across.
