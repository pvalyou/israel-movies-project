The Galil – A Region of Diversity\nThe Galil is a lush, hilly, mostly non-urban region in northern Israel, where Jews, Christians, Muslims, Druze, Circassians, Arabs, and Bedouins live in harmony.\nIt is a unique cultural and ecological landscape where community life thrives across ethnic, religious, and historical lines.\nFilmmakers from all of these communities are involved in the Galil Film Fund’s work—making the festival a microcosm of coexistence and creativity.\n## Unique Venues in Natural Settings\nScreenings and professional events are held in boutique wineries, artist-run spaces, cultural centers, and historical sites throughout the Galil.\nThese intimate, character-rich venues allow for deeper exchange and authentic connection—far from conventional urban venues, and deeply embedded in the region’s natural and cultural setting.\n![Galil Film Festival audience watching projected
