Lapid](https://thejerusalemfilmfund.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Policeman_2011.jpg)\n### Plot Summary\nYaron, an elite special operations squad leader, is the spiritual leader and alpha male among his peers, a small, highly trained team that is part of the Israeli Defense Ministry’s Anti-Terrorism unit. These men work, play, laugh, and cry together. They are true patriots. Yaron’s wife is expecting their first child, and the failing health of a team member weighs on his soul, and the price that must be paid as a result of this. As the story shifts, the antagonists are introduced: a small group of politically extreme individuals with their own vision of how Israeli society should be.   \n\n### Prizes and Awards\nSan Francisco
