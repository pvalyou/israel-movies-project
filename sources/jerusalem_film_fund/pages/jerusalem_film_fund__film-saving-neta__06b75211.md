Plot Summary\nFour women whose lives are in turmoil encounter a mysterious stranger, Neta, whose honesty and vulnerability will help them reconnect with their dearest ones, their families.  \nNeta’s life took a sudden wrong turn and he’s searching for the path again. His story is told through the women’s stories he meets over the course of one year at a crucial point – for them and for him. The women he meets will reroute his life as he will help them reconnect with their dearest.
