General Information\nA big hostel of 300 beds in private rooms, family rooms or dorms. As an institution that set for itself a goal of having travelers from around the world meet and socialize, (and offers social activity on a daily basis) The interior design emphasizes the shared spaces- a welcoming lobby with a library, a rooftop with lounge areas and the main shared space at the first floor with dining tables, self-catering kitchen, a bar, a pool table, hammocks and more.
