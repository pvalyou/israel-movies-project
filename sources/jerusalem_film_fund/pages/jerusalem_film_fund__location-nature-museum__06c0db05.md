and models related to the natural world.  \nAnother smaller building, also two stories high, has no exhibition at the moment but a few classes of various sizes.  \nAt the large yard a few sculptures of dinosaurs, a small petting zoo and a lovely garden (managed by a different organization). \nTags: \n[19th century](https://thejerusalemfilmfund.com/tag/19th-century/),
