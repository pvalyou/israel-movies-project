Appendix B – Recognized direct professional production expenses 
 
1.  Employment of manpower/staff/creators/professional meeting the following Israeli 
professional associations: 
The Association of Artists of Animation (Registered association [RA).), the Association 
of Film and Television Workers (RA), the Directors' Association (RA), the Israel 
Association of Film and Television Screenwriters (RA), the Editors Association, The 
Israeli Players 'Association, the Producers' Association and/or the Israeli Academy of 
Film and Television. 
2. Service providers in the following fields, who received the Project approval for their 
activity in Jerusalem and/or are registered on the Project's site: 
Computerized studios for performing CGI works including animation, special effects, 
image editing studios, sound design studios, photography studios. 
3.   Purchase of goods and / or services that have a direct connection with the execution of 
the Production, from suppliers and service providers operating in Jerusalem in the fields 
of the profession. 
For this purpose, expenses such as accommodation, catering, payments to agencies 
located in Jerusalem such as insurance, fuel, car rental and flight offices will not 
be approved as "professional expenses." 
It should be clarified that the committee has discretion not to approve a budget item, if 
the Committee believes that the budget item does not comply with the purpose of this 
procedure or does not fulfill the purpose of the aid distribution according to this 
procedure or according to the government resolution. The Committee shall specify in 
writing the reasons for its decision.
