The two close friends become social activists determined to assist women like themselves and to provide a better future for next generations. Efrat Shalom Danon and Gili Danon’s documentary displays the contrasts between Israeli and German immigration policies, while shedding light on the two women’s mutual fate: an unstable life controlled by government policy, always depending on human kindness, guided by the belief that despite everything, their dreams may still have a chance.\n**Directors** : Efrat Shalom Danon, Gili Danon\n**Script** : Efrat Shalom Danon, Gili Danon\n**Producers** : Osnat Trabelsi, Efrat Shalom Danon, Gili Danon, Co-producer: Sorin Manu\n**Editing** : Gili Danon\n**Filming** : Gili Danon\n**Soundtrack** : Rotem Dror\n**Music** : Ellyott\n55 דקות | Israel, Germany | 2019 | English, Hebrew, German, Tigrinya \n**![](https://kerenmakor.org.il/wp-content/themes/kerenmakor-new-style/sks-inc/sks-img/festival-frame.png)Festivals and awards**\n**Jerusalem Film Festival, Israel, 2019**\n## More in Documentary\n### [Agnes Keleti- Conquering Time **Noemi Schory** ](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/agnes-keleti-conquering-time/?lang=en)\n### [Block of Clay **Roey Victoria Heifetz, Zohar Melinek Ezra** ](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/block-of-clay/?lang=en)\n### [Life in Stills **Tamar Tal Anati** ](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/life-in-stills/?lang=en)\n![](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/an-ordinary-life/?lang=en)\n\n\n---\n## Links\n\n\n### Internal\n- [Agnes Keleti- Conquering Time\n                                        Noemi Schory](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/agnes-keleti-conquering-time/?lang=en)\n- [Block of Clay\n                                        Roey Victoria Heifetz, Zohar Melinek Ezra](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/block-of-clay/?lang=en)\n- [Life in Stills\n                                        Tamar Tal Anati](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/life-in-stills/?lang=en)"}
