Assem passed away at the young age of 29 due to cancer. During the 1970s, Assem left the village to study in Tel Aviv lining in poverty and faced difficulty obtaining papers for his drawings, mainly the Katkus as a symbol of Palestine. Both painters Assem and karim are from the Abu-Shakra family of Um-al-Fahm, which has raised five talented painters over the course of 50 years.
