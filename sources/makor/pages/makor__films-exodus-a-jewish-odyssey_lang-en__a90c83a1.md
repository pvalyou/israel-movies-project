After World War II, when more than half of the Jewish people was systematically exterminated by the Nazi extermination machine, the “Exodus” affair arrived and changed the face of history. On a rickety illegal immigrant ship, 4,500 men, women and children, who survived the gas chambers in the extermination camps, were crammed onto its deck, embarking on an unforgettable journey that ultimately led to victory over the British who ruled the Land of Israel.
