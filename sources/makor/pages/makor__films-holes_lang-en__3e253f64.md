The moment Danny received the message about the murder of Itzik, his only son, his life would never again be the same. Ten years of legal proceedings and new disclosures made during that time have slowly crumbled his faith in gaining justice. Along with the pain of his loss, he realizes that only he can find the truth of what happened that night. His journey in search of the truth reveals his complex identity for the first time: a spy who worked for the security services, an Arab who converted and became a practicing Jew, a man who felt pride, respect and sympathy for a country that has absurdly and horrifyingly betrayed him.\n**Directors** : Shirly Berkovitz\n**Script** : Niv Hachlili\n**Research** : Niv Hachlili\n**Producers** : Zahi Cohen\n**Production company** : Shirly Berkovitz\n**Editing** : Sitri Metcalf, Elad Davidovitch Shicowich\n**Filming** : Dror Lebendiger, Matanya Tausig, Uri Ackerman, Avner Shahf\n**Soundtrack** : Avi Mizrahi\n**Music** : Alberto Shwartz\n80 minutes | Israel | 2021 | Hebrew \n**![](https://kerenmakor.org.il/wp-content/themes/kerenmakor-new-style/sks-inc/sks-img/festival-frame.png)Festivals and awards**\n**Docaviv.
