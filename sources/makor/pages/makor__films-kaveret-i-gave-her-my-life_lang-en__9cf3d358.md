It follows Kutner’s personal story that has never been told before—how he lost his memory at age 18, his rehabilitation process which involved constantly listening to music, and how attending a Kaveret concert, his first show after losing his memory, forever changed the course of his life.\nIn the series, Kutner unravels the secret of Kaveret. Now that all the “children” are in their 70s, the band look back openly and honestly, revealing themselves in ways they never have before. Through previously unseen personal materials, we understand how deeply Kaveret is part of the Israeli bloodstream. We will learn the band’s story from its beginning in 1973 until its breakup in 1976, through its various reunions, and understand how it became, over the years, a canon of popular music in Israel.\n**Directors** : Jonathan Avrahami, Avida Livny\n**Script** : Jonathan Avrahami, Avida Livny\n**Research** : Jonathan Avrahami\n**Producers** : Naomi Shamai, Udi Kadishzon\n**Production company** : Kadishzon Productions LTD\n**Editing** : Yoni Cohen\n**Filming** : David Zarif\n**Animation** : Adam Lewensohn\n**Actors** : Danny Sanderson, Gidi Gov, Alon Oleartchik, Meir Fenigstein, Yoni Rechter, Efraim Shamir, Yitzhak Klepter\n**Soundtrack** : Ophir Lokay\n**Music** : Kavert members & other performers\n3 Episodes, 52 minutes each | Israel | 2025 | Hebrew \n**Produced by**\n  * Kan – Israeli Public Broadcasting Corp |\n  * Mifal HaPais - Council for the Culture and Arts \n\n\n## More in Documentary\n### [KICHKA: Telling Myself **Gad Aisen** ](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/kichka-telling-my-self/?lang=en)\n### [A Minor Crime **Nur Fibak** ](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/%d7%94%d7%a8%d7%a3-%d7%94%d7%aa%d7%97%d7%aa%d7%95%d7%9f-%d7%91%d7%99%d7%95%d7%aa%d7%a8/?lang=en)\n### [A Fool’s Dream **Daniel Sirkin** ](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/a-fools-dream/?lang=en)\n![](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/kaveret-i-gave-her-my-life/?lang=en)\n\n\n---\n## Links\n\n\n### Internal\n- [KICHKA: Telling Myself\n                                        Gad Aisen](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/kichka-telling-my-self/?lang=en)\n- [A Minor Crime\n                                        Nur Fibak](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/%d7%94%d7%a8%d7%a3-%d7%94%d7%aa%d7%97%d7%aa%d7%95%d7%9f-%d7%91%d7%99%d7%95%d7%aa%d7%a8/?lang=en)\n- [A Fool’s Dream\n                                        Daniel Sirkin](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/a-fools-dream/?lang=en)"}
