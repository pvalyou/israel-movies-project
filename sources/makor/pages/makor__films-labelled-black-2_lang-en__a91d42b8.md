The experience tells the story of Banchi Mashsasha, an Israeli of Ethiopian descent whose life turned upside down in 2015 after the body of her brother Yosef was found at the bottom of a deserted quarry not far from the winery where he worked. The tragedy that haunts Banchi began four months earlier on a Saturday evening when Yosef was arrested after being attacked without cause by police officers with a taser gun while sitting with a friend on a bench in a playground. The experience oscillates between the realistic and the fantastic, blending the chronicle of events that led to Yosef death with Banchi’s transformation into a prominent protest leader.
