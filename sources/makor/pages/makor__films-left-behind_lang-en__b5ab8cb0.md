Qui Che (14) was left by his mother with his grandparents.. He was 4 years old when his father tragically took his own life due to their financial situation, He grew up lonely with unbearable burdens to help his grandparents with farm work.. He vents his difficulties by writing diaries that provides a glimpse to his inner world. His story reveals a personal journey, filled with emotional, familial, and societal challenges especially the absence of his mother who returns home once in three years.
