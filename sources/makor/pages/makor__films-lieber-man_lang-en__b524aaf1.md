But Lieberman himself, who immigrated to Israel at the age of 20 without a penny to his name, remains a mystery. How did he make it? Is he corrupt or a victim? A racist or a pragmatist? And what lies beneath his turbulent special relations with Benjamin Netanyahu, dating back to the eighties?
