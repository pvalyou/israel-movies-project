His sons, Tzvika and Eyal, continue this demanding work. When Saba Yishai is hospitalized, granddaughter Adva begins to carefully document the family’s journey for over a decade, observing and reflecting on their profound connection to bees, nature, and their interdependence. Through her lens, she witnesses their experiences with uncertainty, aging, death, and renewal.
