He is 75 years old, recently retired, and he feels that in Paris, he has almost nothing but Pierluigi, his partner for the last 45 years. When I entered their apartment during my last visit, I immediately noticed that the old Chairs are gone. ‘I’m selling them” said Reuven. “I do not need chairs dragged behind me when I go to heaven…” Clearly it involved more than just selling chairs. Pierluigi is 10 years younger than Reuven. For him Israel is not an option and he is fighting Reuven’s pessimism and sadness.
