Nidal works to support his family and to cover the medical expenses of his sister who has leukemia. Nidal acts like a tough man that has taken upon himself the responsibility of supporting his family. He arrives to Israel illegally, without any basic personal belongings, manages to escape from the Israeli soldiers and finally succeeds in finding work at the marketplace. His relationship with the Jewish Boss, Hartselle is complicated, and even a deadly terrorist attack by one of his fellow villagers on Israel – doesn’t not hinder the relationship between the two and they remain friends. Despite that, Nidal does not see his future at the Marketplace like many of his friends do. He dreams of becoming a pilot like his uncle in Finland.
