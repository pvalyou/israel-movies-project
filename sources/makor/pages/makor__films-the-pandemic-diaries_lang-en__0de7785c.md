From within isolation, ten of Israel’s leading documentary filmmakers turned their cameras inward, observing family life, the home, the street, and the limits of the permitted 100-meter radius — and occasionally beyond. Edited from footage shot by the filmmakers themselves over a two-month period, this unique project documents their lived realities as they search for their distinctive documentary perspectives amid social distancing and confinement. Filmed across multiple locations — from northern Israel to the south, Jerusalem, Nazareth and Tel Aviv, as well as Italy and New York — the series follows the weeks of global lockdown and examines how Israelis, wherever they were, confronted this unprecedented moment. Themes of migration, religion and politics, anxiety, ideas of home, birth and children, love, freedom and nature converge into a single, collective documentary work.\n**Directors** : (In Hebrew alphabetical order): Chalil Efrat, Enan Barakat, Nir Dvorkin, Tomer Hyman, Zohar Wagner, Ronen Zaritzky, Tamar Tal-Anati, Yael Kipfer, Itamar Roz, Dan Shadur, Abigail Shperber\n**Script** : Liran Atzmor\n**Producers** : Vivi Halpern-Orly Topel\n**Production company** : Kol-miney Hafakot\n**Editing** : Ido Muchrik\n7 Episodes, 15 minutes each | Israel | 2020 | Hebrew \n**Produced by**\n  * yes Docu \n\n\n## More in Documentary\n### [Lebanon – Borders of Blood **Duki Dror, Itai Landsberg, Reinhardt Beetz** ](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/lebanon-borders-of-blood/?lang=en)\n### [Schocken- a self-made man **Noemi Schory** ](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/schocken-a-self-made-man/?lang=en)\n### [The Wounded Healer **Yochay Rosenberg** ](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/the-wounded-healer/?lang=en)\n![](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/the-pandemic-diaries/?lang=en)\n\n\n---\n## Links\n\n\n### Internal\n- [Lebanon – Borders of Blood\n                                        Duki Dror, Itai Landsberg, Reinhardt Beetz](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/lebanon-borders-of-blood/?lang=en)\n- [Schocken- a self-made man\n                                        Noemi Schory](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/schocken-a-self-made-man/?lang=en)\n- [The Wounded Healer\n                                        Yochay Rosenberg](https://kerenmakor.org.il/films/the-wounded-healer/?lang=en)"}
