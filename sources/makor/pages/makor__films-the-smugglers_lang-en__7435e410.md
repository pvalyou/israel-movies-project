Facing mounting challenges in running the shop, he calls upon his nephew, film producer **Tony Copti,** to partner with him. While looking to buy books for the shop, Tony ends up on an eye-opening journey that forces him and his uncle to become book smugglers. The process brings him to question where Jaffa’s books have disappeared to after the 1948 war surrounding Israel’s creation, known to Palestinians as the Nakba. “The Smugglers” is an absurd reflection of Israel’s ongoing oppression of Arabic language and Palestinian culture.\n**Directors** : Tony Copti, Yaniv Berman\n**Script** : Tony Copti, Yaniv Berman\n**Research** : Youssef
