For the fourth consecutive year, the Makor Media Lab incubator has engaged 7 creative teams in connecting stories about Jewish life in Arab and Islamic countries with experimental and narrative immersive creations in new media.\nThe teams deepened their familiarity with the world of XR while enjoying the knowledge and experience of 5 mentors in scriptwriting and storytelling, technology, production, marketing and distribution. All of this, in order to continue developing their projects towards final submissions.\nThree of the seven projects will receive production grants of 433,000 NIS and participate in the XR Development Market at the NewImages Festival in Paris 2026.
