A new kind of modern day avant-guard is drawing audiences to embrace new narrative possibilities, which leverage new technologies and produce unique visual aesthetics, active and interactive actions on social networks, on mobile phones, tablets and TVs, in VR and AR, in art spaces and festivals and in cinemas and theaters. Makor Media Lab is working to identify and nurture a community of multi-disciplinary creators and technologists who are doing innovative work with the art and form of the story.  \n  \n\n## NewImages
