באנגלית.\n## מהרו להירשם, מספר המקומות מוגבל ל-20 משתתפים.\n# **[טופס הרשמה](http://bit.ly/NFCT_Masterclass_Michelle-Greenberg-Kobrin2018)**\n**Masterclass for Filmmakers – Know Your Legal Rights**  \nKnowledge is power. Get to know the rights that you have as a creator of a film, how can these rights be monetized and how you can work with other copyrighted materials like music and archival materials. After serving as the Dean of Students at Columbia Law School, Michelle Greenberg-Kobrin became the director of Cardozo’s Indie Film Clinic, a not-for-profit clinical program that provides free legal services to independent and documentary filmmakers. Join this rare masterclass that empowers you with laws and regulations around copyright, fair use and how to utilize archival material in international distribution.\n**Michelle Greenberg-Kobrin**  \nPROFESSOR AND DIRECTOR  \nTHE INDIE FILM CLINIC AT CARDOZO LAW SCHOOL  \nMichelle Greenberg-Kobrin is a Clinical Professor of Law at Cardozo Law School, where she teaches and directs the Indie Film Clinic, which provides pro bono legal services to filmmakers and video journalists. She is also the Founding Director of the Program on Leadership at the Heyman Center for Corporate Governance. Previously, Michelle served as Dean of Students at Columbia Law School, where she currently serves as Lecturer-in-Law. Michelle teaches in the areas of intellectual property, transactional law, corporations, negotiation, and leadership.
