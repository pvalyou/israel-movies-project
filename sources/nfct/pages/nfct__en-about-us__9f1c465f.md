Since 1994, we have helped bring more than 1,000 films to life, including documentaries, narrative features, and TV dramas that reflect the rich mosaic of Israeli society and are shown both in Israel and overseas.\nWe believe that film has a unique power to inspire dialogue, foster empathy, and drive social progress while sustaining a vibrant creative industry that supports thousands of Israeli families.\n**Our Mission**  \nNFCT is committed to supporting outstanding Israeli cinema that combines artistic excellence, fresh perspectives, and social relevance. We invest in both emerging and established filmmakers across the country, ensuring that Israeli storytelling remains innovative, daring, and diverse.\n**How We Work**  \nEach year, we fund dozens of new projects through a rigorous, values-driven selection process. We prioritize artistic merit, originality, and the ability to engage audiences in a meaningful way. Alongside our main funding tracks, we spearhead initiatives that advance equal opportunities in the film industry, such as labs and special programs supporting women filmmakers, Arab creatives, ultra-Orthodox women, and other communities historically underrepresented on screen.\n**Our Values**\n  * **Excellence and Innovation**  \nWe seek projects with strong creative vision, distinctive cinematic language, and cultural or historical significance.\n  * **Diversity and Inclusion**  \nWe strive to give voice to as many sections of Israeli society as possible, portraying its complexity in all its facets.\n  * **Collaboration and Support**  \nBeyond funding, we provide professional guidance, mentorship, and connections to investors and distributors.\n  * **Social Impact**  \nWe believe films can be catalysts for education, empathy, and positive change.\n\n\n**Looking Ahead**  \nNFCT remains dedicated to championing bold and original Israeli cinema, work that not only entertains, but sparks conversation and builds bridges across communities.\n[**Explore our film catalogue**](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive/)  \n[**Donate to support independent Israeli
