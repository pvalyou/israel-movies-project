To date, 121,995 people have been deported and another 192,412 suspects reviewed. The new anti-immigration sector is decked with a massive annual budget of about 170 million Israeli Shekels, 473 officers and around 90 vehicles referred to by the persecuted foreigners as “Fifty Two Fifty.” With the help of a professional crew of volunteers, armed with cameras, we latched on to the “Fifty Two Fifty” cars to film these officers in action.
