His large oil paintings are treasured by collectors worldwide.  \nIt was director Ben Shani’s encounter with one of Shamir’s works that spawned the idea of documenting the artist at work. Neither of them had any idea that everything would change as the filming progressed, as an unforeseen danger threatened to rob Shamir of his talent.  \nFilmed over the course of ten years, A lullaby for the Valley focuses on the fascinating figure of Eli Shamir and his paintings.
