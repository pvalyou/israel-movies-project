Despite his plight he chooses life. Rinat, his former girlfriend, decides to get back together with him, despite his condition. She wants to make sure he can father children, but it turns out she’s the one who’s barren. And Einav, a single mother who decides to become a surrogate mother for Rinat and Elad.
