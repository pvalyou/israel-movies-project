My Mother the Architect](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2024/07/%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%94-%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%AA-WEB-604x340.jpg)\nAda Karmi Melamede is one of the most prolific architects of her generation in Israel and abroad. Through the lens of her daughter’s film camera lens, an extraordinary protagonist is revealed whose life story is intertwined with the story of the turbulent and complicated country she loves. Ada offers an unusually evocative perspective on ideas such as “place,” “home,” and “state” as someone working toward improving the public sphere while that sphere deteriorates and becomes increasingly fraught. As so many are shaken by the fragility of the democracy we call home, Ada gives us ways to think about what we seek in our personal and public lives, and the spaces and places in between.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nJerusalem Film Festival, Israel, 2024\n**Director** : Yael Melamede\n**Producer** : Hilla Medalia, Yael Melamede\n**Production Company** : Medalia Productions\n**Cinematographer** : Daniel Kedem, Guy Raz\n**Editor** : Sharon Yaish\n**Soundtrack Editor** : Yossi Epelbaum\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, yes Docu, Keshet Fund, The Azrieli Foundation, The Jaffe Foundation, Jewish Story Partners, Israel Film Center\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [עדה - אמי האדריכלית](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%d7%a2%d7%93%d7%94-%d7%90%d7%9e%d7%99-%d7%94%d7%90%d7%93%d7%a8%d7%99%d7%9b%d7%9c%d7%99%d7%aa/)\n**Category** : [Art and Culture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Art%20and%20Culture) / [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Family) / [History](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=History) / [Identity](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Identity) / [Women](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Women)\n**Tag Words** : [Ada Karmi Melamede](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Ada%20Karmi%20Melamede) [Architercture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Architercture) [democracy](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=democracy) [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Family) [Israeli High Court](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Israeli%20High%20Court) [mother-daughter relationship](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=mother-daughter%20relationship)\n**Share** : 00New\nAda Karmi Melamede is one of the most prolific architects of her generation in Israel and abroad.
