docu-drama starring Leora Rivlin and featuring animation, telling the little-known story of Ada Sereni: The Lady in the Black Dress. Who was she? And why have we never heard of her?\nAda was the first woman to command the Mossad LeAliyah Bet in Italy – a secret intelligence organization that would later become the Mossad. Her skills, courage, and determination, allowed thousands of Holocaust survivors to sail to Israel aboard the clandestine immigrant ships. Ada was forgotten and erased from the pages of history.
