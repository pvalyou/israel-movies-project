English\n\n\nTwo brothers, Izak and Shepsel, were born in a displaced persons camp after World War II. They lived their entire lives in the shadow of secrets kept from them by the people closest to them. The brothers were separated as babies, neither was told the other existed. An investigation into the mysterious history of their birth family led to an amazing reunion after six decades. The film offers a rare glimpse into the displaced persons camps in post-World War II Germany, showing the vibrant and sometimes wild social life that flourished among the young survivors. This period has hardly been dealt with on screen until now.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nAudience Award at Docaviv Film Festival, Israel, 2016; Hot Docs Festival, Toronto Canada, 2016\n**Director** : Alon Schwarz\n**Producer** : Alon Schwarz\n**Production Company** : Reel Peak Films\n**Cinematographer** : Uriel Sinai, Jonathan Weitzman, Shaul Schwarz, Christina Clusiau\n**Editor** : Halil Efrat\n**Soundtrack Editor** : Aviv Aldema\n**Original Soundtrack** : Yaniv Fridel, Ofer Shabi\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, yes-docu, SWR,arte, MyHeritage, Claims Conference\n**Script** : Halil Efrat, Alon Schwarz\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [הסודות של איידה](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%99%D7%93%D7%94/)\n**Relevant Links:**\n[Film's Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/aidassecrets/) [Film Review The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2017/10/19/movies/aidas-secrets-review.html)\n**Category** : [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Family) / [History](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=History) / [Holocaust](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Holocaust) / [Identity](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Identity) / [Immigration](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Immigration)\n**Tag Words** : [Camps](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Camps) [Displaced Persons](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Displaced%20Persons) [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Family) [family secrets](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=family%20secrets) [reunion](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=reunion) [survivors](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=survivors)\n**Share** : 006\nTwo brothers, Izak and Shepsel, were born in a displaced persons camp after World War II.
