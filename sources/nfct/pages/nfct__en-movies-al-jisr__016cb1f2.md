The film focuses on the stubborn struggle of a group of young, single women, trying to lead the village to a better place. These women struggle with tradition, family hierarchy and often with their own womanhood. This is a story of a long lasting survival attempt of a unique village, estranged from the Israeli society, the Arab society and its own location at the sea shore.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nInternational Women's Film Festival, Israel, 2006\n**Director** : Ibtisam Mara'ana\n**Producer** : Yossi Leon, Noam Shalev\n**Cinematographer** : Avigail Sperber\n**Editor** : Zohar Sela\n**Original Soundtrack** : Ori Ophir\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television\n**Subtitles** : English, Hebrew\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [אל – ג'יסר](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%90%D7%9C-%D7%92%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%A8/)\n**Relevant Links:**\n[Director's website ](http://www.ibtisamfilms.co.il/en-us/films/details/Al%5EJisr) [Review in Now Toronto](http://www.nowtoronto.com/news/story.cfm?content=152202) [Film's page on IMDB](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0956088/)\n**Category** : [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Family) / [Human Rights](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Human%20Rights) / [Israeli Society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Israeli%20Society) / [Minorities](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Minorities) / [Women](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Women)\n**Tag Words** : [Arab society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Arab%20society) [discrimination](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=discrimination) [Israeli society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Israeli%20society) [Jisr E-Zarqa](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Jisr%20E-Zarqa) [poverty](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=poverty) [tradition](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=tradition) [village](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=village)\n**Share** : 0017\nA rare glimpse into the life of the residents of the village Jisr E-Zarqa, who cope with daily poverty, deprivation, discrimination and alienation.
