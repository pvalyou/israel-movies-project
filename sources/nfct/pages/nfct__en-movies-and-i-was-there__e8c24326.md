But what happens when the young people are Israeli soldiers, when the living room is owned by a Palestinian family that is locked up in one on the rooms of the house? 18 years after serving in the army, Eran Paz finds a box of videotapes with rare footage of himself and his squadmates, invading Palestinian homes in the Territories.\nNow Eran sets out on a journey in the footsteps of the people,
