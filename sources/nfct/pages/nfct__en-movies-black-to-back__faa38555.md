It is a 5 channel video installation, showing 5 women making an “Amy Winehouse Hairdo”. Together they keep spreading Winehouse’s figure through the internet, long after her 2011 physical death in. The women are “taken” out of the internet and are given a prestigious stage to perform on’ while their videos are played backwards, letting them take down their hair and return to their original unique look.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nPrint Screen Festival, Israel, 2014\n**Director** : Ayelet Albenda\n**Producer** : Ayelet Albenda\n**Editor** : Tomer Rubens\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television,\n**Script** : Ayelet Albenda\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [Black to Back](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/black-to-back/)\n**Category** : [Art and Culture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Art%20and%20Culture) / [Experimental Cinema](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Experimental%20Cinema) / [Identity](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Identity) / [Women](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Women)\n**Tag Words** : [Amy Winehouse](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Amy%20Winehouse) [Hair](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Hair) [Imitation](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Imitation) [memory](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=memory) [Net](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Net) [Print Screen 2014](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Print%20Screen%202014) [women](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=women) [YouTube](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=YouTube)\n**Share** : 00New\nBlack to Back deals with replication and imitation, as they are manifested in Youtube video comments.
