A creaky- voiced woman confides her personal account of the Armenian genocide. A sweetly deluded old Casanova still tries to charm and seduce. A blind photographer fiddles with his flash as he points his own camera back at us. This playful, immaculately controlled film finds hypnotizing rhythms in the residents’ limbo-like state. Meanwhile, outside, ominous construction equipment transforms the land.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nLocarno Festival - Special Mention Award, \"Filmmakers of the Present\", Switzerland, 2017;
