He is 13, and he always was a little different. People tried to tame him. They hurt him quite a bit. “I was a fat kid who loved to daydream and draw fairies instead of playing soccer, and no one wanted to play with me. They bullied me, and then the monsters came out.” It was out of this pain that he discovered the power of art. He’s been drawing monsters ever since, though he later added other characters as well. At age ten, he defied a world that pushes us to perfection by creating the iconic character “Botox”, and became part of the artistic community that chooses the street as its canvas.  \nArmed with the belief that art will save the world, just like it saved him from himself, he discovers the hope and strength hidden deep inside him.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nDocAviv Festival, Isral, 2016;\n**Director** : Julie Shles\n**Producer** : Yoav Leshem\n**Production Company** : Beyond Creative\n**Cinematographer** : Avner Shahaf\n**Editor** : Sharon Yaish\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, The Second Authority for TV & Radio\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [אל תקרא לי חמוד](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%90%D7%9C-%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%90-%D7%9C%D7%99-%D7%97%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%93/)\n**Category** : [Art and Culture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Art%20and%20Culture) / [Children and Youth](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Children%20and%20Youth) / [Identity](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Identity) / [Israeli Society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Israeli%20Society)\n**Tag Words** : [Abuse](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Abuse) [courage](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=courage) [creative](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=creative) [street art](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=street%20art) [strength](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=strength)\n**Share** : 00New\nHe calls himself TRA.
