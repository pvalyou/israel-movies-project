Gradually, and not without difficulties, they open up to this woman who suddenly “invades” their world, and provide fascinating insights into the world of a group of 35-45 year old men: how they view male identity, friendship, family and what women really mean to them. Here is an amusing look at Israeli male society as seen from a totally unfamiliar perspective – the perspective of a woman.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nLeipzig Film Festival, Germany, 1996; Paris Film Festival, France, 1996; Colombia Film Festival, 1996; Barcelona Film Festival, Spain, 1996; Hong Kong Film Festival, 1996; Input (International Public Television Conference), 1996; Israefest, New York, USA, 1996; Femina, Germany, 1996; Jewish Cinema, Washington, USA, 1996; London Film Festival, UK, 1997\n**Director** : Michal Aviad\n**Producer** : Amit Goren\n**Cinematographer** : Ron Katzenelson\n**Editor** : Tali Halter-Shenkar\n**Original Soundtrack** : Doron Shenkar\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, Channel 1 -
