They run a dairy farm that is a microcosm of the outside world, and that brings together three seemingly disparate men who are actually very similar: Yossi, an Israeli, has owned the farm for nearly fifty years; he works alongside Ibrahim, a Bedouin from Gaza, and Eddie from Thailand.
