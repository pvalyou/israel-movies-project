This is a series about people and their emotional experiences. The games in the series function as a framework for personal stories about facing difficulties and challenges. Besides being gamers, many of the protagonists in the series come from communities that sometimes feel alienated by Israeli society (immigrants, LGBT people, minorities, people with disabilities, and more).
