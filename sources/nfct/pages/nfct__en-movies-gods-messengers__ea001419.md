receive updates about what's going on at the NFCT\n# God’s Messengers\n  * **Documentary**\n  * /\n  * 76 min.\n  * /\n  * 2015\n  * /\n  * Israel\n  * /\n  * Hebrew, Arabic\n\n\nThis film contains rare documentation of the Hilltop Youth of Gilad Farm, the most radical Jewish outpost in all the West Bank. Due to American and European pressure, the threat of evacuation hangs over their heads. They confront the Israeli government, its security forces, and the Palestinians, and pose a rebellious, sometimes violent and lawless challenge to their foes, always guided by an ideology that knows no compromise. As they see it, they are God’s representatives on Earth.  \nThe settlement was founded by Itay and Bat-Zion Zar in 2002 to avenge the murder of Itay’s brother. Although it was dismantled on several occasions, Gilad Farm was never abandoned.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nDocAviv International Film Festival, Israel, 2015\n**Director** : Itzik Lerner\n**Producer** : Itzik Lerner, Nir Toib\n**Production Company** : Pakada Films\n**Cinematographer** : Yaron Vienstien, Daniel Gal\n**Editor** : Itzik Lerner, Gil Izakov\n**Soundtrack Editor** : Yonatan Mulian\n**Original Soundtrack** : Isar Sulman\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, IBA - Channel 1\n**Script** : Itzik Lerner\n**Distributor** : Ruth Diskin Films\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [שליחי האל](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%97%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9C/)\n**Category** : [Borders and Periphery](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Borders%20and%20Periphery) / [Israeli-Palestinian Conflict](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Israeli-Palestinian%20Conflict)\n**Tag Words** : [Gilad Farm](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Gilad%20Farm) [Hilltop Youth](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Hilltop%20Youth) [Ideology](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Ideology) [Israeli government](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Israeli%20government) [Jewish setllers](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Jewish%20setllers) [palestinian](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=palestinian) [radical](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=radical) [settlement](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=settlement) [Violence](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Violence) [West Bank](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=West%20Bank)\n**Share** : 0029\nThis film contains rare documentation of the Hilltop Youth of Gilad Farm, the most radical Jewish outpost in all the West Bank.
