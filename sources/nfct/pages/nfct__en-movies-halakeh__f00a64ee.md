Yoni, once a barely observant Jew, is now devoted to the passionately Hassidic Breslov tradition. His wife Yael has remained in a more moderate, mainstream branch of Judaism. Yiftach’s third birthday falls on the holiday of Lag Ba’omer, and Yoni wants to take Yiftach through a masculine rite of passage – the halakeh ceremony, where a boy’s hair is cut for the first time, leaving only his ear-locks. The long journey to Mount Meron brings their conflict into sharp relief. Their encounters with various characters along the way further highlight the chasm which has developed between them.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nNominated for Best Drama in the Israeli Academy Awards, 2009; Jerusalem International Film Festival, 2008; International Women`s Film Festival, Rehovot, 2008; Austin Jewish Film Festival, 2009; Limmud NY, 2009; ONfilm Fest, Northfolk, Virginia, 2009; Brazil Religion Film Festival, 2009; Signis Special Mention at the Religion Today Film Festival, Trento, Italy 2010\n**Director** : Avigail Sperber\n**Producer** : Gil Mezuman\n**Soundtrack Editor** : Jonathan Bar-Giora\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, IBA\n**Script** : Mordechai Vardi\n**Co-Production** : Mordechai Vardi\n**Distributor** : Go2Films\n**Subtitles** : English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [חלאקה](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%97%D7%9C%D7%90%D7%A7%D7%94/)\n**Relevant Links:**\n[Interview in Forward Magazine](http://forward.com/articles/103526/a-test-of-faith/) [IsraelFilmBlog](http://israelfilm.blogspot.co.il/2010/04/halakeh.html)\n**Category** : [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Family) / [Identity](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Identity) / [Judaism and Jewish Culture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Judaism%20and%20Jewish%20Culture) / [Women](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Women)\n**Tag Words** : [Breslov](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Breslov) [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Family) [Lag Ba'omer](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Lag%20Ba'omer) [marriage](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=marriage) [Mount Meron](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Mount%20Meron) [Religion](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Religion)\n**Share** : 00New\nThis is the story of a journey made by a young religious family: Yael, Yoni, and their three-year-old son Yiftach.
