Israel, recognized as the world leader in drip-irrigation technology, shares its innovative technologies with countries around the world – but, on the ground, things don’t always go as expected. Filmmaker Jonathan Paz follows Arbel on this cinematically colorful journey – a journey that is by turns frustrating, exciting, surreal and funny. For the charismatic and driven Arbel, it is also an inner journey deep into himself.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nToronto Jewish Film Festival, Canada, 2013; Jerusalem International Film Festival,
