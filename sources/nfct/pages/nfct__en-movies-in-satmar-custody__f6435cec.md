The story exposes a deep cultural gap between the Yemenite families and the Yiddish Satmar Community that became distractive and tragic to families who have traveled thousands of miles to an entirely different planet of their own, with strange rules, norms, morals and lifestyles. Still in Yemen, Yemenite Jewish families are brainwashed by skillful missionaries, unable to defend themselves in the eye of this intricate and deceptive operation. The film follows the life of Yahia and Lauza Jaradi who were brought from Yemen into the Satmar Community.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\n[Full list of festivals](http://www.ruthfilms.com/films/docs/israeli-mosaic/in-satmar-custody.html \"festivals\"); Audience Award at the Marseille Documentary Film Festival, France, 2003; Alaska Jewish Film Festival, USA, 2007; L.A.
