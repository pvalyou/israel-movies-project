Hebrew\n\n\n![](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2014/04/Lyrics-by-Rothblit.jpg)\nThe five discs that Yankeleh Rothblit put out during the period of 1978-2011 tell the story of a dual life; a dialogue with the daily reality of Israeli society alongside the personal development of an artist. The Journey to Peace examines these parallel lines. Yankeleh Rothblit rose to fame mostly as a result of texts that he wrote and that were set to music and performed by others. These songs, as beautiful as they are, tend to be on the lighter side. They are not especially complex or demanding of the listener in the way of his independent work. The film exposes the audience to the entirety of his work – from the writing of the texts, to the composition of the music and to the performance of his own songs. His is a rich, complex and thought-provoking oeuvre.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nThe Haifa International Film Festival, Israel, 2012\n**Director** : Deva Oz Melman\n**Producer** : Yehuda Bitton, Leat Kamay Ached\n**Production Company** : Yehuda Bitton Productions\n**Cinematographer** : Meny Elyas, Deva Melman\n**Editor** : Dana Shalev\n**Original Soundtrack** : Devek, Yankeleh Rothblit\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, The Second Authority for TV and Radio, Chanel 10\n**Script** : Deva Melman, Ron Ofer, Danny Varta\n**Distributor** : Yehuda Bitton Productions\n**Subtitles** : English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [מילים בסרט/יענקל'ה רוטבליט](https://nfct.org.il/movies/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%A1%D7%A8%D7%98-%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%A0%D7%A7%D7%9C%D7%94-%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%98%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%98/)\n**Category** : [Art and Culture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Art%20and%20Culture) / [Israeli Society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Israeli%20Society)\n**Tag Words** : [art](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=art) [composer](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=composer) [Music](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Music) [singer](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=singer) [songwriter](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=songwriter) [Yankeleh Rothblit](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Yankeleh%20Rothblit)\n**Share** : 00New\nThe five discs that Yankeleh Rothblit put out during the period of 1978-2011 tell the story of a dual life; a dialogue with the daily reality of Israeli society alongside the personal development of an artist.
