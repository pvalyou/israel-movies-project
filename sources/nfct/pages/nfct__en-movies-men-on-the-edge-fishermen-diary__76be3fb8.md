The film depicts them as men of two nationalities, who work together against the elements, thereby forming a unique intimacy, men fighting something much greater than themselves, relying on each other for their lives.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nBest Documentary at the Mediterranean Film Festival, Portugal, 2006;
