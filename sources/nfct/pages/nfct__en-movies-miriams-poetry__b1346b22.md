She died halfway between Tel Aviv and Jerusalem, at the age of 46, in a car accident that looked more like a suicide. In her life and poetry she wandered restlessly, in buses, taxis and hitchhiking, feeling out of place. In a rare recording from 1988, discovered by accident, Miri reads a touching love letter, dedicated to the poet Meir Wieseltier. This love letter constitutes the heart of the film and exposes a central axis of her poems and her wounded soul.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nEpos Art Film Festival, Tel Aviv, Israel,
