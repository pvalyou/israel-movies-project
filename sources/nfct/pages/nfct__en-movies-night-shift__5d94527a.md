It was never officially defined, but when you look at the history of the neighborhood, you discover that virtually every decade there has been a population turnover in Kiryat Moshe and it has almost always remained homogeneous – Holocaust survivors, followed by immigrants from Arab countries, Russian immigrants, and in the past 20 years – Ethiopians. The neighborhood contains the largest concentration of Ethiopian immigrants in the country.\nThe story of the neighborhood is told during the night shift of the local cop. Rafi Mells, an Ethiopian beat cop, is the only person in charge of the neighborhood, but unlike most cops, whose task is to maintain law and order, Rafi’s work revolves around helping the residents of Kiryat Moshe adapt to life in Israel.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nHaifa International Film Festival - Special Mention, Israel, 2013\n**Director** : Itamar Chen\n**Producer** : Yoav Roeh, Aurit Zamir\n**Production Company** : Gum Films\n**Cinematographer** : Philippe Bellaiche\n**Editor** : Arik Lahav-Leibovitz\n**Soundtrack Editor** : Sharon Luzon\n**Original Soundtrack** : Ronnie Reshef\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Teleivison, Mifal ha Pais yes Docu\n**Script** : Itamar Chen, Arik Lahav-Leibovitz\n**Distributor** : Go2Films\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [משמרת לילה](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%94/)\n**Category** : [Aliyah](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Aliyah) / [Borders and Periphery](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Borders%20and%20Periphery) / [Israeli Society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Israeli%20Society) / [Minorities](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Minorities) / [Old Age](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Old%20Age)\n**Tag Words** : [Ethiopian ghetto police](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Ethiopian%20ghetto%20police) [social issues](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=social%20issues)\n**Share** : 004\nOne neighborhood, one cop, one night.  \nThe Kiryat Moshe neighborhood of Rehovot is undoubtedly the most homogeneous neighborhood in Israel, always was.
