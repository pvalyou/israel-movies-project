The son of Yehezkel Henkin, founder of HaShomer organization, he was raised against the backdrop of the Galilean hills at a time when the Turks and then the British ruled the country. At the age of 80, Yossi, the director’s grandfather, reacquaints himself with the landscapes that shaped his life from the day he was born.
