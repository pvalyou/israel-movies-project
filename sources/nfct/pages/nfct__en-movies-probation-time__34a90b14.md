At the same time, her religious family in Jerusalem is struggling with the “demons” threatening to overwhelm their daughter Ariella. Ariella is an Ethiopian girl, who was adopted by Avigail’s parents, Rabbi Daniel and Hannah Sperber, when she was four. She is the tenth child, with nine older siblings, each of whom chose a unique path to follow. But Ariella forces the family to contend with problems of another sort: alcoholism, petty thefts, and prison. Does the story of Ariella have an inevitable ending? Can she accept and absorb the love and concern that surround her? And will her family be able to love her unconditionally? This is a depiction of the fragile bonds that make up a family.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nBest Documentary Film and Best Cinematography at DocAviv Film Festival,Israel, 2014; IDFA, the Netherlands, 2014; Outfest - the LGBT Film Festival, Los Angeles, USA, 2015\n**Director** : Avigail Sperber\n**Producer** : Executive Producer - Ilanit Baumann, Producer- Enav Shenar\n**Production Company** : Endemol Israel\n**Cinematographer** : Avigail Sperber\n**Editor** : Ido Haar\n**Soundtrack Editor** : Aviv Aldema, Sharon Luzon\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television;The Avi Chai Foundation, Yes Doco\n**Musicians** : Ronit Roland\n**Script** : Avigail Sperber\n**Distributor** : Go2Films\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [תקופת מבחן](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%91%D7%97%D7%9F/)\n**Category** : [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Family) / [Judaism and Jewish Culture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Judaism%20and%20Jewish%20Culture) / [LGBT](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=LGBT)\n**Tag Words** : [daughter](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=daughter) [Ethiopia](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Ethiopia) [girlfriend](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=girlfriend) [life partner](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=life%20partner) [parents](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=parents) [Rabbi](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Rabbi) [religious](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=religious) [teen](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=teen)\n**Share** : 00New\nAvigail, the director of this film, is dealing with a break-up from her girlfriend and with raising a child that she had with her.
