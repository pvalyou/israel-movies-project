To whom do they belong?\nIn 1982, The Israeli army invaded Beirut. A film archive of the PLO’s filming unit, containing hundreds of hours of raw materials, disappeared.\n“SHALAL” is a story of a lost Palestinian film archive, delving into three historical events captured in the footage. It is the story of the Israeli–Palestinian conflict—from a new viewpoint.
