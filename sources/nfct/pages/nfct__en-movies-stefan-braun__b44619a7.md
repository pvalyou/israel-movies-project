Day in and day out he sits in Stefan’s room which hasn’t been touched since Stefan passed away, recording himself talking to him. Eliezer has recorded hundreds of tapes in which he tells Stefan about the events of the day, sings to him, and includes him in his thoughts and longings. Eliezer Rath and Stefan Braun’s love story began in the 1950’s in a young conformist state where homosexuality was outlawed, in Stefan Braun’s colorful and successful fur salon frequented by affluent residents and visitors from around the world.However, for Stefan’s family, the relationship between the two is not an amazing and harmonious love story. They describe Eliezer as someone who rapidly transformed from lover to servant, ready to endure humiliation and betrayal just so he could be near his lover, while Stefan became the master totally dependent on his servant’s close service.After Stefan’s death, Eliezer Rath is forced to prove his love for his life-partner. He is confronted by Stefan’s close-knit family who express suspicions about Stefan’s last days in light of the will he left behind. Conversations held between Eliezer and Stefan’s family, rare recordings, personal diaries, hundreds of stills and old 8mm films tell the fascinating and complex love story of furrier Stefan Braun and tailor Eliezer Rath.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nBest Promising Director at The Israeli Documentary Forum Awards, 2008; The Cinematography Award at The Jerusalem International Film Festival, Israel, 2007; Wisconsin Madison Jewish/Israeli Film Festival, USA, 2009; Santa Fe International Film Festival, USA, 2008; Washington Jewish Film Festival, USA, 2008; Seattle Lesbian & Gay Film Festival, USA, 2008; Berlin Jewish Film Festival, Germany, 2008; UK Jewish Film Festival, UK, 2008; San Francisco Jewish Film Festival, USA, 2008; New York International Independent Film and Video Festival, USA, 2008; New York Jewish Film Festival, USA, 2008\n**Director** : Itamar Alcalay\n**Producer** : Amir Harel, Ayelet Kait, lee levi\n**Production Company** : Lama Films\n**Cinematographer** : Itai Neeman\n**Editor** : Joelle Alexis\n**Original Soundtrack** : Ronit Rolland\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, NOGA Communications -
