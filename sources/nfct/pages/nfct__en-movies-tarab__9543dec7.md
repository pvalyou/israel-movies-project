Boris Maftsir sets out to trace the memories and personal identity of his friend Eli, and in the process encounters his own buried memories from the distant past in the Former Soviet Union. TARAB is a heightened spiritual and sensual state induced by Arabic music. TARAB is the music that ‘Fuad Elias’ heard as a young boy in Baghdad before becoming the renowned and respected Israeli author, Eli Amir. And TARAB is also the way in which Maftsir gains entry into the inner world of his eldest daughter Orit, one of the world’s most well known belly dancers.\n**Director** : Boris Maftsir\n**Producer** : Zvi Shefi\n**Production Company** : Zvi Shefi Productions\n**Cinematographer** : Yoram Millo, Ron Calderon\n**Editor** : Lilia Zvakevnekal Alexander\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, Channel 1 - Israel Broadcasting Authority, Pais Council for Arts and Culture\n**Script** : Eli Amir, Boris Maftsir\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [טאראב](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%98%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%91/)\n**Category** : [Art and Culture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Art%20and%20Culture) / [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Family) / [Identity](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Identity)\n**Tag Words** : [Arabic](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Arabic) [author](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=author) [Eli Amir](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Eli%20Amir) [former Soviet Union](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=former%20Soviet%20Union) [Fuad Elias](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Fuad%20Elias) [Journey](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Journey) [Music](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Music) [Soviet Union](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Soviet%20Union)\n**Share** : 00New\nThe Journey of author Eli Amir and Filmmaker Boris Mafstir in search of identity and memory.
