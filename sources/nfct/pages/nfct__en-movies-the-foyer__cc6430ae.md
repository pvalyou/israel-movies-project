entrance to the Tel Aviv Museum is the exposition — with which begins the visit to the museum. It is a place of gathering and discourse, a space dedicated to artistic expression and events. Each work hanging in it demonstrates a fascinating chapter in the museum’s collective memory and history. THE FOYER inspects the artistic choices made in the museum’s foyer over the years, and through them attempts to analyze the changes this artistic institution has undergone, as well as its cultural influences.  \nProduced in the framework of a special joint film project of the NFCT and the Tel-Aviv Museum of Art, in celebration of the Museum’s 90th anniversary.\n**Director** : Michal Houminer, Ron Katzenelson\n**Producer** : Shula Spiegel, Dana Eden\n**Production Company** : Donna and Shula studios\n**Cinematographer** : Ron Katzenelson\n**Editor** : Yuval Orr\n**Soundtrack Editor** : Neal Gibbs\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, Tel Aviv Museum of Art\n**Colourist / Online Editor** : Aharon Peer\n**Visual Effects** : Ayala Sharot\n**Script** : Michal Houminer, Ron Katzenelson\n**Subtitles** : English, Hebrew\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [הפואיה](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%d7%94%d7%a4%d7%95%d7%90%d7%99%d7%94/)\n**Category** : [Art and Culture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Art%20and%20Culture)\n**Tag Words** : [90th anniversary](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=90th%20anniversary) [art](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=art) [Artistic Choice](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Artistic%20Choice) [Cultural Institution](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Cultural%20Institution) [Curation](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Curation) [Entrace](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Entrace) [Roy Lichtenstein](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Roy%20Lichtenstein) [Tel Aviv Museum of Art](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Tel%20Aviv%20Museum%20of%20Art)\n**Share** : 00New\nThe entrance to the Tel Aviv Museum is the exposition — with which begins the visit to the museum.
