What seemed at the beginning of the evening as a harmless party game quickly becomes destructive as personal secrets come out. Each of the sentences is a confession of adultery, whether truthful or not, and each of the participants uses the game to his or her own manipulative needs. By the end of the evening, the biggest secret is revealed, the one after which, nothing will ever be the same again.
