The Bedouins, who have lived there for over century, serve as scouts in the IDF. They have been living in tin huts since 1963, without electricity, rationed water and an asphalt road. Despite the village being officially recognized in 1999, the bureaucratic red tape of government offices and political considerations have delayed the construction permits. Fahim, the protagonist of the film, strives to combat the injustices faced by his community and to secure access to essential services.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nOther Israel Film Festival, New York, 2010; Nominated for the CMCA International Prize for Mediterranean Documentary and Reportage, Italy, 2010; Doc Aviv International Documentary Film Festival, Israel 2010\n**Director** : Gil Karni\n**Producer** : Gil Karni\n**Production Company** : J.M.T Films\n**Cinematographer** : Ariel Glikson\n**Editor** : Rachel Yagil\n**Soundtrack Editor** : Yossi Appelbaum\n**Original Soundtrack** : Shlomo Mizrachi\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [הבלתי נראים](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%94%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%99-%D7%A0%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9D/)\n**Category** : [Human Rights](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Human%20Rights) / [Israeli Society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Israeli%20Society) / [Minorities](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Minorities)\n**Tag Words** : [Arab al-Na'im](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Arab%20al-Na'im) [Arab minority](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Arab%20minority) [Bedouin](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Bedouin) [Galilee](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Galilee) [IDF](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=IDF) [unrecognized village](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=unrecognized%20village)\n**Share** : 00New\nThe film documents a period of twelve years in the unrecognized village of Arab al-Na’im in the Galilee.
