The apartment belongs to young couple Shir and Rami. Shir is woken up one evening by the doorbell. An angry neighbor hands over the dog Rami had taken out earlier in the evening. Rami has suddenly disappeared. The film follows the events in the apartment during the night, where a series of visitors join the woman waiting: friends, family, the police, and unexpected guests. In the powerful dialogues and occasionally fierce confrontations, humiliating secrets come to the surface.
