receive updates about what's going on at the NFCT\n# The Way Home\n  * **Documentary**\n  * /\n  * 8 X 34 Min.\n  * /\n  * 2009\n  * /\n  * Israel\n  * /\n  * Hebrew\n\n\nPassing time fills a central role in The Way Home. Using old 8 mm. films and an intimate portrayal of the significant events in his personal life over the last fifteen years, director Tomer Heymann (It Kinda Scares Me, Paper Dolls, Bridge Over the Wadi), reveals a charged and surprising emotional world. Shooting with a small video cam gives the film a feeling of great intimacy with the main characters and the director himself, who deals with personal, familial, and national crises through his obsessive filming, constantly seeking his way back home.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nBest Documentary Series Award at the Jerusalem International Film Festival, Israel, 2009; Best Documentary Series Award at the Filmmakers Forum Competition, Israel, 2010\n**Director** : Tomer Heymann\n**Producer** : Barak Heymann, Tomer Heymann\n**Production Company** : Heymann Brothers Films\n**Cinematographer** : Tomer Heymann\n**Editor** : Ron Goldman\n**Original Soundtrack** : Israel Bright\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, Yes Docu\n**Musicians** : Theme song by: Rona Kenan\n**Script** : Tomer Heymann, Ron Goldman\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [בדרך הביתה](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%91%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%91%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%94/)\n**Relevant Links:**\n[Official Website](http://heymannfilms.com/film/the-way-home/ \"website\")\n**Category** : [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Family) / [LGBT](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=LGBT)\n**Tag Words** : [8 mm](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=8%20mm) [archival footage](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=archival%20footage) [intimacy](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=intimacy) [personal journey](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=personal%20journey)\n**Share** : 00New\nPassing time fills a central role in The Way Home.
