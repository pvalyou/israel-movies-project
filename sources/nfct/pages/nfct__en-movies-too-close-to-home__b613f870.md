Once they start talking about money, we’ll be called greedy, and when the struggle against the withdrawal begins, we’ll be considered traitors.” That’s what five residents of the Golan Heights said during a month-long stint of reserve duty in the Golan during the winter. For a month they reflect on the lives they’ve built for themselves in the Golan Heights, while change looms on the horizon and the peace talks are progressing and an agreement would mean returning the Golan Heights to Syria. This is a humorous and touching film about people who take themselves and their predicament with a pinch of salt, but cannot conceal the pain.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nJerusalem International Film Festival, Israel, 1995; Israfest, New York, USA, 1995\n**Director** : Ori Inbar\n**Producer** : Ori Inbar\n**Production Company** : Elrom Films Ltd.\n**Cinematographer** : Uzi Livne\n**Editor** : Sasha Franklein\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television\n**Subtitles** : English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [שני מטר מהבית](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%98%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%94%D7%91%D7%99%D7%AA/)\n**Category** : [Borders and Periphery](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Borders%20and%20Periphery) / [Israeli Society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Israeli%20Society) / [Zionism](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Zionism)\n**Tag Words** : [Golan Heights](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Golan%20Heights) [IDF](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=IDF) [peace talks](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=peace%20talks) [Pioneers](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Pioneers) [reserve duty](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=reserve%20duty) [settlers](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=settlers) [soldiers](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=soldiers) [Syria](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Syria) [traitors](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=traitors) [winter](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=winter)\n**Share** : 00New\n“When we came here, we were pioneers, later we became residents, and we’ve already been called settlers.
