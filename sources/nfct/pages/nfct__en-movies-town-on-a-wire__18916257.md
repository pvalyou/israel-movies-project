Acute poverty has helped to cultivate racism, bigotry, and violence, so that many of Lod’s 75,000 inhabitants – Jewish, Muslim, and Christian alike – live in a constant state of fear and misery. Filmmakers Uri Rosenwaks and Eyal Blachson delve deep into the entrails of this small, hardened city.
