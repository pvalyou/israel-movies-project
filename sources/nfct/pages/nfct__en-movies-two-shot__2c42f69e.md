After many years of failed attempts, Naomi decides to talk to her father in the only language he understands – the language of film. She puts a camera in front of him, and through their meetings Naomi tries to understand the reasons behind this difficult relationship. Meanwhile Menachem begins to reveal his unique production methods, his dreams, and the difficulties he faces.
