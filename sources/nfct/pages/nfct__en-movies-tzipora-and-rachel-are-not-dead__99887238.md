Together, the two sisters challenge and dismantle language – both verbal and cinematic – until once again it becomes a tool they can use to speak the silence, the oblivion, the dissociation: the black abyss of trauma, what it is they cannot remember and therefore, unable to forget. It is an encounter that takes its participants, if they are willing to join this harrowing journey, back to the starting point; to uncharted, and therefore, terrifying territories of the psyche.  \nA 16-year documentation of a struggle with the existence of a raw traumatic excess, which cannot be spoken or mediated verbally.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nDocaviv Film Festival 2023, Tel Aviv,
