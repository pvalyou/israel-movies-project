concept of reincarnation, while both mysterious and enchanting, is hard for most Westerners to grasp. _Unmistaken Child_ follows the 4-year search for the reincarnation of Lama Konchog, a world-renowned Tibetan master who passed away in 2001 at age 84. The Dalai Lama charges the deceased monk’s devoted disciple, Tenzin Zopa (who had been in his service since the age of seven), to search for his master’s reincarnation. Tenzin sets off on this unforgettable quest on foot, mule and even helicopter, through breathtaking landscapes and remote traditional Tibetan villages.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nBest Documentary Award - Haifa International Film Festival, 2008; Full Frame Inspiration Award - Full Frame Film Festival, 2008; Best Documentary - RiverRun International Film Festival, 2008; Grand Prix and Audience Award at EBS International Film Festival, 2008\n**Director** : Nati Baratz\n**Producer** : Nati Baratz, Ilil Alexander, Arik Bernstein\n**Cinematographer** : Yaron Orbach\n**Editor** : Ron Goldman\n**Original Soundtrack** : Siril Morrin\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television; NOGA Communications - Channel 8; BBC; ARTE\n**Subtitles** : Hebrew, English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [הגלגול](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%94%D7%92%D7%9C%D7%92%D7%95%D7%9C/)\n**Relevant Links:**\n[Official website ](http://www.unmistakenchild.com/index.php) [Review in the _New York Times_](http://www.nytimes.com/2009/06/06/movies/06chil.html?_r=0) [Film's page on IMDB](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1286798/)\n**Category** : [International Stories](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=International%20Stories) / [Religion](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Religion)\n**Tag Words** : [Tibet; Buddhism; reincarnation; Dalai Lama](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Tibet;%20Buddhism;%20reincarnation;%20Dalai%20Lama)\n**Share** : 00New\nThe Buddhist concept of reincarnation, while both mysterious and enchanting, is hard for most Westerners to grasp. _Unmistaken Child_ follows the 4-year search for the reincarnation of Lama Konchog, a world-renowned Tibetan master who passed away in 2001 at age 84.
