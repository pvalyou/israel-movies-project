The movie tracks the connections between her life story and her thinking, through rare archival footage demonstrating the Banality of Evil. Her Ideas on the nature of Evil, pluralism, Freedom, etc., are relevant today more than ever.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nJerusalem International Film Festival, Israel, 2015; Special Mention and Best
