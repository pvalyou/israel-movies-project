Throughout the generations this place has been home to a variety of immigrant populations, first to Jewish immigrants from North Africa and Eastern Europe and later from Ethiopia and Russia. Eventually, displaced Palestinian Arabs came to live in the place known as “the dump” as well. Told in three instalments each filmed ten years apart, the film chronicles the changes that occurred in Wadi Rushmia, a microcosm of Israeli society, as well as the changes undergone by the city of Haifa overall.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nJerusalem IFF ,2001; Venice ,2001; Filmax Tokyo, 2001; Sao Paulo, 2001; Boston, 2001\n**Director** : Amos Gitai\n**Producer** : Amos Gitai\n**Cinematographer** : Nurith Aviv, Jorge Gurvitch\n**Editor** : Ifat Feirberg\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, Noga Communications – Channel 8, ARTE\n**Distributor** : broadcast on ARTE\n**Subtitles** : English, Hebrew\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [חיפה עירנו](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%95/)\n**Relevant Links:**\n[Amos Gitai's official website ](http://www.amosgitai.com/html/film.asp?docid=32&lang=1)\n**Category** : [Israeli Society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Israeli%20Society)\n**Tag Words** : [Haifa](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Haifa) [Microcosm](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Microcosm) [Wadi Rushmia](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Wadi%20Rushmia)\n**Share** : 00New\nOn the eastern skirts of Haifa sits Wadi Rushmia, a region of abandoned quarries from the days of the British Mandate.
