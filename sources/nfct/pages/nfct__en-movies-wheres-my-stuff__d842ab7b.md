Hebrew\n\n\n![](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2014/04/wheres-my-stuff.jpg)\nThe loneliness and solitude of the individual within his family is exposed through the relationship between the director and his elderly mother.  \nThe mother suffers from a severe case of obsessive hoarding. The situation worsens when a Palestinian carpenter from East Jerusalem warms his way into the heart and daily life of the mother and causes turmoil within the already crumbling family unit. The family must pull together to try and rescue the mother from her self-destructive tendencies.  \n_Where’s My Stuff_ documents the stubborn argumentative reality of a family that is capable of showing warmth and compassion at the same time.  \nThe line between parent and child becomes blurred as the story unfolds and the film pushes boundaries and raises questions about ageing, honor and voyeurism.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nThe Haifa International Film Festival, Israel, 2011\n**Director** : Zohar Kaniel\n**Producer** : Zohar Kaniel\n**Production Company** : Here and Now Films\n**Cinematographer** : Zohar Kaniel\n**Editor** : Nadav Harel\n**Original Soundtrack** : Niv Golan\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, Noga Communications - Channel 8\n**Subtitles** : English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [איפה הדברים שלי](https://nfct.org.il/movies/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%93%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%99/)\n**Relevant Links:**\n[The film's page on IMDB ](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2033466/ \"IMDB\")\n**Category** : [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Family) / [Old Age](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Old%20Age)\n**Tag Words** : [East Jerusalem](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=East%20Jerusalem) [elderly](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=elderly) [Family](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Family) [hoarding](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=hoarding) [mother](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=mother) [palestinian](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=palestinian) [relationship](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=relationship)\n**Share** : 00New\nThe loneliness and solitude of the individual within his family is exposed through the relationship between the director and his elderly mother.  \nThe mother suffers from a severe case of obsessive hoarding.
