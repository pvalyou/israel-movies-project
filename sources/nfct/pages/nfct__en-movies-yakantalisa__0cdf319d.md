receive updates about what's going on at the NFCT\n# Yakantalisa\n  * **Documentary**\n  * /\n  * 79 Min\n  * /\n  * 1996\n  * /\n  * Israel\n  * /\n  * Hebrew\n\n\nIn May 1994, one of the most significant voices in the Israeli cultural scene was silenced abruptly. Hezi Laskaly, a poet, artist, choreographer, and critic, died of AIDS at the age of 42. In February 1996 a film crew set off on the trail of the words he left behind. “Yakantalisa” is a cinematic portrait of a poet, an attempt to extricate his secret and an unyielding effort to uncover the genetics of beauty itself. Laskaly understood that life and art demanded their “pound of flesh,” and as a total artist, who obscured the boundaries between the two, he paid a heavy price.\n**Festivals and Awards:**\nJerusalem International Film Festival, Israel, 1996; Jewish Cinema, Washington, USA, 1996; Berlinale, Germany, 1996\n**Director** : Yair Lev\n**Producer** : Yael Shavit\n**Cinematographer** : Itzik Portal\n**Editor** : Era Lapid\n**Original Soundtrack** : Ziv Gola\n**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television, The Cinema Project\n**Subtitles** : English\n**Film Name in Hebrew** : [יקנטליסה](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%94/)\n**Relevant Links:**\n[The film's IMDB page](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0159839/ \"IMDB\")\n**Category** : [Art and Culture](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Art%20and%20Culture) / [Health](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Health) / [Israeli Society](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?category=Israeli%20Society)\n**Tag Words** : [AIDS](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=AIDS) [artist](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=artist) [choreographer](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=choreographer) [critic](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=critic) [Death](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Death) [Hezi Laskaly](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Hezi%20Laskaly) [HIV](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=HIV) [Poet](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=Poet) [tragedy](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies-archive?keywords=tragedy)\n**Share** : 00New\nIn May 1994, one of the most significant voices in the Israeli cultural scene was silenced abruptly.
