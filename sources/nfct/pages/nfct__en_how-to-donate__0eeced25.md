# How to donate
**The NFCT needs your support in order to advance Israeli society through the art of cinema. With each donation we get one step closer to achieving that goal.  
****Thank you in advance for your support****!**
* * *
## **For donors based in the U.S**
**You are welcome to make a tax-deductible contribution via our fiscal sponsors****:**
*** “P.E.F. Israel Endowment Funds” –**
  * **Credit card donations** : to make a credit card donation, please click [here](https://web.causematch.com/donate/nfct-israel) and you will be taken to NFCT’s CauseMatch donate page (they work with P.E.F).
  * **Bank wire transfer** : if you wish to make a wire transfer, please send an email to: info@pefisrael.org in order to receive instructions and bank details from P.E.F
  * **Check donations** : if you prefer to make a check donation, please make out a check to “P.E.F. Israel Endowment Funds” and send it by mail to:  
P.E.F. Israel Endowment Funds  
630 Third Avenue, Suite 1501  
New York NY 10017


**We request that donors specify the donation is recommended to be used for the New Fund for Cinema and Television (NFCT), non-profit number 58-0234870, and if your donation is for a specific NFCT program or project, please mention this too.**
P.E.F. will authorize the donation, forward it to the NFCT and send the donor a receipt.
* **[JGIVE](https://www.jgive.com/new/en/usd/collect/donation-targets/137222/amount)****Credit card**(in USD, CAD, GBP and ILS)
* * *
## **For donors based in the UK**
**You are welcome to make a tax-deductible contribution via our fiscal sponsors** :
***[ JGIVE](https://www.jgive.com/new/en/gbp/collect/donation-targets/137222/amount)** – **Credit card**(in USD, CAD, GBP and ILS).  
*** NIF** –
  * **Online:** please visit [https://newisraelfund.org.uk/donate-directly/](https://newisraelfund.org.uk/donate-directly/). Please add the NFCT in the Comments (Gift Detail) box.
  * **Bank wire transfer** [[View PDF](https://www.nif.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/NIF-Grantee-Resources_4_UK-WireTransferInformation_June2021.pdf)]: Please email NIF UK at info@uknif.org, and include your full name, the NFCT’s name (and program/project name if applicable) and your email and/or postal address for the receipt. Please add “NFCT” to the transfer reference.
  * **Check donations:** please make out a check made payable to the New Israel Fund and send it to:  
New Israel Fund  
Star House  
104-108 Grafton Rd  
Kentish Town  
London NW5 4BA.


**You must add your name and a cover note stating that the donation is donor-advised to the NFCT and a postal/email address for the receipt.**
**_IMPORTANT_ : To ensure that your donation is correctly identified and the gift correctly attributed to the NFCT, please make sure that you send NIF UK all the details stated above. **NIF will authorize the donation, forward it to the NFCT and send the donor a receipt.
*** UK Toremet** **– Bank wire transfer** from the UK.  
Please contact Ms. Irit Shimrat at**irit@nfct.org.il **to learn how.

Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
