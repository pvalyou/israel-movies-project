# A Unicorn in Tel Aviv
  * **Student**
  * /
  * 20 Min.
  * /
  * 2019
  * /
  * Israel
  * /
  * Hebrew


![A Unicorn in Tel Aviv](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2019/12/final-poster_english-1-604x846.jpg)
Alonna (28), a romantic who clings to old habits, tries to beat Tel Aviv. The city fights back with cyclists, parking inspectors, and guys who don’t believe in love. Losing hope, she downloads a dating app. Her date with “Mr. Perfect” seems promising, but an unexpected incident changes everything.

Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
