An old tape reveals a practical joke that went terribly wrong. The revelation leads to an unexpected search – not only for the prankster, but for the filmmaker himself.
**Festivals and Awards:**
Best Short Film, Israeli Academy Awards, 2015; Best Student Fiction Film at the Jerusalem International Film Festival, Israel, 2014; Special Mention - the Film Critics Forum Awards at the Tel Aviv International Student Film Festival, Israel, 2014

Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
