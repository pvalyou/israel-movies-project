# Cherries

  * /
  * 14 Min.
  * /
  * 2013
  * /
  * Israel
  * /
  * Hebrew


![](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2015/06/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-1-604x339.jpg)
From `Short:Long` – a tribute to Israeli cinema. A Tribute to Lemon popsicle – 1978. The Sabbath is a few hours away. Victor, a confirmed ultra-Orthodox Jew, is stuck in cosmopolitan Tel Aviv after missing the last bus home. Seeking to find his way out, he encounters a different everyday reality that evokes long forgotten feelings.
**Festivals and Awards:**
Tel Aviv International Student Film Festival, Israel, 2013
**Director** : Noa Osheroff
**Producer** : Efrat Choen
**Cinematographer** : Moses Mishali
**Editor** : Eitan Vardi

Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
