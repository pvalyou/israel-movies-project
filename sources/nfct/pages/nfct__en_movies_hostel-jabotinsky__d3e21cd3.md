# Hostel Jabotinsky
  * **Documentary**
  * /
  * 60 Min.
  * /
  * 2026
  * /
  * Israel


![Hostel Jabotinsky](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/05/%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%98%D7%9C-%D7%96%D7%91%D7%95%D7%98%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99-604x349.jpg)
In the heart of Tel Aviv, between crowded parking lots and behind a pink fence, stands Jabotinsky Hostel, home to 23 adults on the autism spectrum. Adi, a staff member at the hostel, documents their lives over the years, both within the hostel and beyond it. Through her lens, a mutual relationship of trust, openness, and honesty begins to form.
Over time, the lives of the residents and Adi’s own personal life become intertwined – shaped by war, separations, and change, alongside moments of laughter, friendship, and dreams fulfilled.
After two decades of a shared life, the Jabotinsky Hostel is about to close. As the farewell approaches, the roles begin to shift, and the distance between both sides of the fence gradually disappears.
**Festivals and Awards:**
Docaviv Film Festival, Tel-Aviv Israel, 2026
**Director** : Adi Yaffe Cohen
**Producer** : Almog Gurevich, Yonatan Nir
**Cinematographer** : Adi Yaffe Cohen
**Soundtrack Editor** : Aviv Aldema
**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund For Cinema and Television, Gesher Multicultural Film, Mifal HaPais Council for the Culture and Arts
