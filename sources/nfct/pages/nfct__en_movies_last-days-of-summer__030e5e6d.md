  * Arabic, Hebrew


![Last Days of Summer](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/07/%D7%94%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%97%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A5-1-604x261.jpg)
Darwish, a young man with existential fatigue, returns home from work with one concern: to fix the broken fan in his house. While his neighbour gets shot and wounded in front of the building and is taken away to hospital, Darwish finds himself stuck in the crime scene looking for solutions for his fan, while police officers investigate the scene.

The New Fund for Cinema and Television (NFCT) is an Israeli non-governmental organization positioned at the intersection of cinema and social change. Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
