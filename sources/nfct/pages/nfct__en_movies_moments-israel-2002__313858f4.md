# Moments, Israel 2002
  * **Experimental**
  * /
  * 56 Min
  * /
  * 2002
  * /
  * Israel
  * /
  * Hebrew, Arabic


![](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2014/03/%D7%9E%D7%91%D7%98%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C-2002.jpg)

Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
