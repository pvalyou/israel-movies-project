# One Eye Open
  * /
  * 55 Min.
  * /
  * 2026
  * /
  * Israel


![One Eye Open](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/05/%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%90%D7%97%D7%AA-%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%97%D7%94-WEB-604x340.jpg)
Inside the snowballing chaos of reality, a reservist soldier finds himself caught between the surrender of the self and a deepening inner crisis. A film about a widening fracture — the inner collapse of a reservist soldier caught in a war with no end in sight. Told from within, it follows the quiet voices of those who answered the call and now endure the public burden: staying on despite injustice, detachment, exhaustion, and despair. A raw, intimate testimony from the heart of the war — from October 7th to the present day.
**Festivals and Awards:**
Docaviv Film Festival, Tel-Aviv Israel, 2026
**Director** : Tom Giat
**Producer** : Ohad Milstein
**Editor** : Tom Giat, Ohad Milstein
**Soundtrack Editor** : Shahaf Wagshall
**Original Soundtrack** : Ishai Adar
**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund For Cinema and Television, Weil Bloch Film Award, The Kadar Foundation for the Advancement of Culture, Art and Science
**Script** : Ohad Milstein

The New Fund for Cinema and Television (NFCT) is an Israeli non-governmental organization positioned at the intersection of cinema and social change. Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
