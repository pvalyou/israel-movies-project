# Out at Six
  * **Documentary**
  * /
  * 52 Min.
  * /
  * 2025
  * /
  * Israel
  * /
  * Hebrew


![Out at Six](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/08/%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%AA%D7%99-%D7%91%D7%A9%D7%A9-WEB-604x272-1.jpg)
A young girl from a good home starts dating a slightly older man, and it is not long before she starts falling out of her other connections and relationships, swallowed up by a closed world that only has two people (and a few big dogs tied to the front door). Although she hasn’t experience violence at home, she fears that if she leaves him, something terrible will happen. _Out at Six_ tells the story of Livi, the filmmaker, through a timeline divided into eight stages based on research taken from hundreds of cases in which women were murdered by their partners. The film is compiled of seires of interviews with the people closest to her, reflecting how the surrounding environment witnesses the reality of such relationship.
