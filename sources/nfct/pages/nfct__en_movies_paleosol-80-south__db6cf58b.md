# Paleosol 80 South
  * **Experimental**
  * /
  * 15 Min
  * /
  * 2013
  * /
  * Israel
  * /
  * Hebrew


![](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2014/02/paleosol-80-darom-604x430.jpg)
A poetic journey in the Israeli desert shot through a thermal camera. In apocalyptic surroundings, detached from time or place, lies a fascinating encounter between ancient mythologies and the political present.
**Festivals and Awards:**
The Jerusalem Film Festival 2013 (The Israeli Video and Art Experimental Film Competition)
**Director** : Amir Yatziv , Jonathan Doweck
**Producer** : Amir Yatziv , Jonathan Doweck
**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund for Cinema and Television

Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
