# Portrait

  * Israel

![Portrait](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2021/11/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%9F-604x319.jpg)
As time draws closer towards the jail release of her violent husband, Kifaya Ayati, a resident of Acre, who has been brutally attacked several times, is looking for a way to survive. She paints women who have been abused and murdered by their spouses and meets their sisters, mothers and daughters. Every meeting becomes a portrait of a murdered woman and an indictment against Israeli society, the police and the courts.
**Festivals and Awards:**
Best Documentary Film Award - Haifa International Film Festiva, Israel, 2021

The New Fund for Cinema and Television (NFCT) is an Israeli non-governmental organization positioned at the intersection of cinema and social change. Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
