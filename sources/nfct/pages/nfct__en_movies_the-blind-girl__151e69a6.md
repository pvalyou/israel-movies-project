# The Blind Girl
  * **Experimental**
  * /
  * 5 Min
  * /
  * 2002
  * /
  * Israel
  * /
  * Hebrew


![](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2014/03/Makom-Project-Short-Cuts-Ramallah-_1-604x398.jpg)
Part of “[The Makom Project](https://nfct.org.il/en/movies/the-makom-project/ "Makom")“:  
A portrait of six people and the short story “From the Journal of a Blind Girl” by Israeli playwright Hanoch Levin, reflect a culture and a place.

Through our financial and professional support to filmmakers, we cultivate artistic excellence and contribute to the creation of outstanding films on a broad array of issues.
