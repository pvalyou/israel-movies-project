# The Partisan With The Leica Camera
  * **Documentary**
  * /
  * 54 Min.
  * /
  * 2022
  * /
  * Israel
  * /
  * Hebrew


![The Partisan With The Leica Camera](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2022/07/%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%99%D7%96%D7%9F-%D7%A2%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94-604x340.jpg)
The story of Mundek Lukawiecki and his wife Hannah Bern, who fought the Nazis with the Polish partisans and hid in the forests. Mundek not only employed his bravery and cunning, but also his Leica camera, giving us a rare glimpse into the life of the partisans.
**Festivals and Awards:**
Jerusalem International Film Festival, Israel, 2022
