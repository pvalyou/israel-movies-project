# The Woman Who Didn’t Know How to Love
  * **Documentary**
  * /
  * 80 Min.
  * /
  * 2026
  * /
  * Israel


![The Woman Who Didn’t Know How to Love](https://nfct.org.il/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/05/%D7%94%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%90-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%90%D7%94%D7%95%D7%91-WEB-604x340.jpg)
Discovering my late grandmother’s personal archive sparks a cinematic journey into the relationship between my mother and me. Through audio tapes, 8mm films, and one present-day encounter, a three-generational dialogue emerges that could never have taken place. The film brings memory, secrets, and conflicting versions of love together, revealing what was passed down without ever being spoken.
**Festivals and Awards:**
Docaviv Film Festival, Tel-Aviv Israel, 2026
**Director** : Rotem Dimand
**Producer** : Mor Trager, Tzipi Baider
**Supporter(s)** : The New Fund For Cinema and Television, Mifal HaPais Council for the Culture and Arts
**Film Name in Hebrew** : [האישה שלא ידעה לאהוב](https://nfct.org.il/blog/movies/%d7%94%d7%90%d7%99%d7%a9%d7%94-%d7%a9%d7%9c%d7%90-%d7%99%d7%93%d7%a2%d7%94-%d7%9c%d7%90%d7%94%d7%95%d7%91/)
