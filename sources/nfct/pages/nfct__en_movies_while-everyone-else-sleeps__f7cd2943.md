# While Everyone Else Sleeps
  * **Documentary**
  * /
  * 62 Min
  * /
  * 2010
  * /
  * Turkey
  * /
  * Turkish


While Everyone Else Sleeps tells the story of an extraordinary taxi driver who takes stunning photographs of homeless people without any training, while he works at nights. His only purpose is to create public awareness for helping those people in need.
**Festivals and Awards:**
Winner of the Golden Orange Award -Best First Documentary at the 2010 Antalya Film Festival in Turkey; International Istanbul Film Festival, 2010; Winner of the Open Eyes Award at the MedFilm Festival 2011 International Competition
**Director** : Ayfer Tokatlioglu
**Producer** : Ayfer Tokatlioglu
**Editor** : E.M Celikler
**Soundtrack Editor** : Oguz Kaynak
**Subtitles** : English
